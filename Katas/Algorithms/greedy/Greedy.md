# Greedy

Greedy is an algorithmic paradigm that builds a problem piece by piece, choosing in that moment the next piece that seems more efficient.

Don't use the divide and conquer; we go right away to the answer, and we make the best decision at the moment. Regardless of the future, don't be the best.
